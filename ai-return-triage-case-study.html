03 / Design

AI should assist the decision, never hide it.

A rules-first service design where the vision model contributes a bounded observation, not an opaque final verdict.

Proposed Claude Vision API architecture. A backend sends a consented return photo with a tightly defined inspection prompt to Claude’s existing Vision API. Its structured observation is combined with deterministic order and policy checks to recommend a next step. There is no custom vision-model training, labelled training set, train/validation split, or accuracy comparison in this concept.

04 / Scope

Trade-offs, not fabricated experiments.

Deliberate product choices define the MVP and make its future evaluation meaningful.

Keep humans in the middle

Exceptions, appeals, disputes, and the 31–65 band reach a trained reviewer.

Trade-offMore handling time and cost for ambiguity.

Make policy deterministic first

Return windows, SKU eligibility, and visible rules are checked before vision assistance.

Trade-offRules need version control and care.

Constrain the vision role

The API produces structured photo observations, not an autonomous customer outcome.

Trade-offQuality, privacy, cost, and consistency need testing.
